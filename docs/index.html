StreamFHIR demos

Citizen stream checks become HL7 FHIR data a health team can read. Five views of the same app, no install. Source: github.com/Ryugi62/streamfhir.

1 · The 60-second demo (synthetic)Mill Pond needs verification: under report SYN-006 a reviewer presses "Confirm · photo checked", and the warning appears. 2 · Real citizen science (FreshWater Watch)25 real volunteer records near Toulouse and in central Portugal. Two "blue-green scum" ticks on flowing water go to a reviewer with the volunteer's photo instead of raising an alarm. 3 · Real agency data: Toulouse (Hub'Eau)52 river stations. La Rivel at Baziège: nitrate 62 mg/L, and "One query, two sources": one EU code returns agency and citizen readings together. 4 · Real agency data: Ghent (VMM)3 stations, 39 samplings, same importer shape. 5 · Real agency data: Benevento (ARPA Campania)6 stations, 32 samplings; pH values given as "n.d." go to a person.
